## The idea

A feature toggle lets you merge code to main and keep it invisible until you're ready.
The deploy ships; the behavior stays off. If something breaks, you flip a switch —
not a revert commit at 4PM on a Friday.

## What I use them for

**Release toggles** hide work-in-progress from users. **Ops toggles** kill a hot path
without redeploying. **Experiment toggles** route a slice of traffic to a new path so
you can compare error rates side by side.

## What I got wrong early

I treated toggles as permanent configuration. They aren't. Every flag needs an owner
and a removal date, or you end up with fifteen layers of `if (flagX && !flagY)` that
nobody dares touch.

## The rule I follow now

Ship dark. Enable in staging first. Enable for yourself in prod. Then a small cohort.
Delete the toggle once the feature is the default — the cleanup is part of the work.
